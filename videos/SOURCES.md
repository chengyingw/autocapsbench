## Visual review and limits

Full-duration five-second interval contact sheet and full-resolution representative frames inspected. Four-panel contact experiment: Si3N4 ball / PTFE@PMMA matrix at 1 N; Si3N4 ball / PTFE@PMMA + PAO/MoDTC@PI composite at 1, 2 and 3 N. The authors describe the movie as oil-releasing processes under interfacial stress stimulation during loading and running-in.

The images show the contact region, not a resolved close-up of an individual capsule shell cracking. Do not present the website mechanism illustration as a reconstruction of visible individual-capsule rupture, or infer film thickness, COF, strain or a physical timescale from the illustration.

The original MP4 is unedited and at the original playback speed. Its codec is HEVC Main / AAC LC, which is not supported by every browser. si002-browser.mp4 is a browser-compatible H.264 (libx264, CRF23, yuv420p, fast-start metadata) transcode at the same 1892 × 1080 resolution and 30 fps, with original AAC audio copied. It preserves all frames, field of view and timing; no cropping, annotations or speed changes. Download links point to the original, checksum-verified HEVC file.

si002-poster.webp is an uncropped full frame at about 15 s, resized to 1000 pixels wide and encoded as WebP. The SVG mechanism animation is original website artwork informed by the paper, separately labeled as a qualitative, not-to-scale illustration. It stops after release/lubrication rather than depicting automatic capsule refilling. Its reset control is an explicit replay of the illustration.
